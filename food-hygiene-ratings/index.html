Food Hygiene Ratings Are Coming: Get Your Kitchen Inspection-Ready

16 Nov 2026 · 2 min read

The Isle of Man's new food hygiene rating scheme is still on track, according to the Department of Environment, Food and Agriculture (DEFA). From early 2027, results will be published online for the first time.

300+cafés, restaurants, takeaways and pubs serving meals to be inspected
0–5rating scale, the same as England, Wales and the Channel Islands
Early 2027when businesses in scope are inspected, in a short window

DEFA says the standards businesses must meet are not changing; what is new is that inspection results will be public. Inspectors will look at how food is handled, the cleanliness and condition of the premises, and whether management systems are in place. The minister is yet to confirm the next steps, so keep an eye on official updates at gov.im/foodhygienescheme.

Our tip: don't wait for the inspector

Walk your kitchen and front of house as if you were one. Ask yourself:

  • Are cleaning schedules written down and signed off?
  • Are hand-wash stations stocked?
  • Are cloths and equipment colour-coded and stored properly?
  • Are your hygiene essentials topped up for the Christmas rush?

DEFA's Environmental Health Team can offer individual advice on preparing. We're also happy to chat through general hygiene supplies, so give the team a call on 01624 671200.
